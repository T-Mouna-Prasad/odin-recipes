Butter Chicken

A beloved, globally recognized Indian curry featuring tender pieces of marinated, spiced chicken. The chicken is simmered in a velvety, mildly sweet, and deeply savory gravy made from tomatoes, butter, and cream.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken thighs (boneless and skinless)
  • 3 tbsp plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 large onion (finely chopped)
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 50ml heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp butter

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken thighs in the yogurt, half of the garam masala, turmeric, and chili powder for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Sear the marinated chicken in a hot pan until browned, then set it aside.
  3. In the same pan, melt half the butter and sauté the onions and ginger-garlic paste until softened..
  4. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and the remaining spices, simmering the mixture for 15 minutes.
  5. Blend the tomato mixture until completely smooth, then return it to the pan.
  6. Stir in the remaining butter, heavy cream, and cooked chicken, simmering gently until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
