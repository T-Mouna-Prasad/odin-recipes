Tacos al Pastor

Inspired by Lebanese immigrants in Mexico, this vibrant street food features thinly sliced pork marinated in an earthy, bright red adobo sauce. It is traditionally cooked on a vertical spit, but this version adapts those iconic sweet and savory flavors for the home kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 500g pork shoulder (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tbsp achiote paste
  • 1/2 cup pineapple juice
  • 1 tbsp chili powder (ancho or guajillo)
  • Small corn tortillas
  • 1 cup fresh pineapple (diced)
  • 1/2 white onion (finely minced)
  • Fresh cilantro (chopped)

Steps

  1. Whisk together the achiote paste, pineapple juice, and chili powder to create the marinade.
  2. Toss the sliced pork in the marinade and refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
  3. Heat a heavy skillet over high heat until smoking hot.
  4. Sear the pork slices in batches until lightly charred and cooked through.
  5. Chop the cooked pork into small, bite-sized pieces.
  6. Warm the corn tortillas in a dry skillet.
  7. Assemble the tacos by placing the pork on the tortillas and topping generously with diced pineapple, onion, and cilantro.
