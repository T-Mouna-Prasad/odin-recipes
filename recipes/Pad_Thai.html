Pad Thai

Thailand’s famous national dish, offering a masterful balance of sweet, sour, salty, and spicy notes. Chewy rice noodles are tossed in a blazing hot wok with a tamarind-based sauce, egg, and fresh aromatics.

Ingredients

  • 200g flat dried rice noodles
  • 200g medium shrimp or sliced chicken breast
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups fresh bean sprouts
  • 1/2 cup roasted peanuts (crushed)
  • 3 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar (or brown sugar)

Steps

  1. Soak the rice noodles in warm water for about 30 minutes until pliable but still slightly firm, then drain.
  2. Whisk the tamarind paste, fish sauce, and palm sugar together in a small bowl to make the sauce.
  3. Heat a wok over high heat with a little oil and stir-fry the shrimp or chicken until just cooked.
  4. Push the protein to the side of the wok, crack the eggs into the empty space, and scramble them quickly.
  5. Add the drained noodles and the sauce to the wok, tossing everything continuously until the noodles absorb the liquid and soften.
  6. Remove from the heat, stir in the bean sprouts, and garnish with crushed peanuts.
